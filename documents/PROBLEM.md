We are solving the gap between having structured ecological evidence and being able to answer a practitioner’s question reliably.

The graph already contains thousands of claims, but a user cannot easily determine which apply to their question, whether studies agree, or whether the evidence supports an answer at all. A language model could produce a convincing response while confusing outcomes, reversing effects, or inventing missing details.

Our objective

Build a Lane 2 assistant that answers:
“According to the supplied evidence, what does intervention or condition X do to outcome Y—and how sure are we?”

Each answer must:
- Identify the correct intervention, outcome, and relevant context.
- Distinguish increases, decreases, null findings, and conflicting evidence.
- Cite the exact supporting claims.
- Communicate evidence strength and calibrated confidence.
- Say “the graph cannot answer this” when appropriate.
Success means accurate answers to unseen questions, faithful use of CAMO, reproducible execution, and low energy consumption.
